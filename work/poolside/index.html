Poolside

A staircase, a walk-in wardrobe and a vanity unit for one house.

Services
Staircases, Wardrobes, Bathrooms
Location
North West
Details
Ash, stained black · Xylocleaf Ares Beton · Matt black ironmongery

An ash staircase designed and manufactured in our workshop, stained black then lacquered, with the glass templated to the strings. Upstairs, a walk-in wardrobe and a floating vanity unit.

The vanity unit is in Ares Beton, a Xylocleaf board, on the handleless Gola profile in matt black to match the rest of the bathroom furniture.

The walk-in wardrobe pairs light boards with matt black rails and knobs, with hanging, shelving and drawers planned around the room.
