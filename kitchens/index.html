What goes into our kitchens

Handleless, on the Gola profile
Doors with no handles, opened on a recessed rail: in matt black, or in brushed brass against matt black doors.
Shaker and in-frame
Shaker kitchens with the doors painted to the colour you choose, for houses that suit something more traditional.
Boards chosen for the house
Xylocleaf boards such as Santos Grey and Blue Lagoon, picked so the kitchen flows with the rest of the interior.
Tall units and larders
Ovens, larders and open shelving built into one wall, with lighting where it helps.
Islands
Islands with seating and wine storage, and worktops that wrap down the ends.
Made in our workshop
Designed with you, manufactured in our workshop and installed by the same team.
